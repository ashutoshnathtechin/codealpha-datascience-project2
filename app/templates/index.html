Crisis Hotspots

Puducherry & High-Service States

Puducherry witnessed an extreme +65.84 percentage point surge, reaching 67.02% unemployment. Tourism, hospitality, and informal retail collapse paralyzed coastal and service economies.

Migrant Labor Corridors (Bihar & Jharkhand)

Jharkhand (+42.93 pp) and Bihar (+34.19 pp) were severely impacted as millions of daily-wage migrants returned from shuttered metropolitan factories, overwhelming local rural labor absorption.

Agrarian Resiliency (Chhattisgarh & Northeast)

States like Chhattisgarh (-1.57 pp), Sikkim (-10.17 pp), and Meghalaya remained remarkably insulated due to high rural agrarian dominance and early localized agricultural exemptions.
